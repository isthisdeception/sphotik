# About Sphotik

**A neighbourhood library, run the modern way**

Sphotik began with a simple idea: good books should not have to be bought every time you want to read them.

So we set out to build a membership library for Bangladesh, a place where you can discover good books, borrow them, read at your own pace, and return them when you're done.

Your membership gives you access to the library. Your refundable deposit determines how much you can borrow. Delivery is separate and always clear.

We believe a library should be simple, honest, and accessible, with no hidden rental fees, no confusing availability, and no unnecessary ownership.

We are starting small and growing deliberately, city by city, shelf by shelf, so we can learn from our readers and make the service better as we grow.
