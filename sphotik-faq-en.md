# Sphotik FAQ

## What is Sphotik?

Sphotik is a membership-based library. Instead of buying every book you want to read, you can become a member, borrow books from our collection, read them, return them, and borrow again.

## What does the membership fee cover?

The membership fee gives you access to the Sphotik library for your chosen membership period.

It does not include the refundable security deposit or delivery charges.

## How much does membership cost?

Our current regular membership prices are:

- **6 months:** ৳1,200
- **12 months:** ৳2,000

During the launch period, founding members can join at special prices:

- **6 months:** ৳900
- **12 months:** ৳1,600

## What is the security deposit?

The security deposit is a refundable amount that protects the library against loss, serious damage, or other outstanding charges.

It is separate from your membership fee and determines your borrowing capacity.

## How much can I borrow?

Your borrowing capacity depends on your security deposit:

| Security Deposit | Books at a Time | Maximum Total Book Value | Borrowing Period |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| ৳500 | Up to 2 books | Up to ৳700 | 10 days |
| ৳1,000 | Up to 4 books | Up to ৳1,400 | 14 days |
| ৳1,500 | Up to 6 books | Up to ৳2,000 | 21 days |

You must stay within both the book-count limit and the maximum total book-value limit.

## Is the security deposit refundable?

Yes. The security deposit is refundable, provided that all borrowed books have been returned and any applicable loss, damage, or other outstanding charges have been settled.

## Can I increase my borrowing limit later?

Yes. You can add to your security deposit and move to a higher borrowing level, subject to the available options and current Sphotik rules.

## Do I pay a rental fee for every book?

No. There is no separate rental fee for each book.

Your membership gives you access to the library. The security deposit determines how much you can borrow.

## Do I have to pay for delivery?

Yes. Delivery is separate from the membership fee.

You pay the delivery charge when books are sent from Sphotik to you, and you pay the return delivery charge when books are sent back to Sphotik.

Multiple books sent together in one shipment count as one delivery trip.

## Which delivery service do you use?

During the initial launch, Sphotik may use private courier services for delivery.

As the service grows, we may introduce additional delivery options, including more economical services in selected areas.

Available delivery options and charges will be shown before you confirm a shipment.

## What does "Available now" mean?

"Available now" means that at least one copy of the book is currently at Sphotik and is available to be prepared and shipped.

A book may be unavailable even if it is part of our library because all available copies are currently being borrowed by other members.

## What happens if a book is unavailable?

When all copies of a book are currently with members, you may be able to join the waitlist.

When a copy becomes available, members on the waitlist can be notified or offered the book according to their position in the queue.

## Can I request a book that Sphotik does not have?

Yes. You can request a book that is not currently in our collection.

We will review requests based on reader interest, availability, price, and other practical considerations. A request does not guarantee that we will purchase the book.

## How long can I keep a book?

Your borrowing period depends on your security deposit level:

- **৳500 deposit:** 10 days
- **৳1,000 deposit:** 14 days
- **৳1,500 deposit:** 21 days

The applicable period will be shown for your borrowed books.

## Can I renew a book?

A renewal may be possible when nobody else is waiting for the book.

Popular or reserved books may not be eligible for renewal.

## What happens if I return a book late?

Late returns can prevent other members from accessing books.

Sphotik may temporarily suspend your borrowing privileges until overdue books are returned. Repeated or extended late returns may result in additional restrictions or charges according to the current membership rules.

## What happens if I damage a book?

Normal signs of reading and reasonable wear are acceptable.

Damage beyond normal use may result in a reasonable repair or replacement charge. Where applicable, the amount may be deducted from your refundable security deposit.

## What happens if I lose a book?

If a book is lost or damaged beyond reasonable repair, Sphotik may charge an appropriate replacement amount.

The amount may be deducted from your security deposit. If the amount exceeds your available deposit, you will be responsible for the remaining amount.

## Can I cancel my membership early?

Yes. You may choose to stop using Sphotik before your membership period ends.

The membership fee is not refunded for the unused portion.

Your security deposit will remain with Sphotik until the original membership period ends. After the original membership period has ended, you can request your eligible deposit refund.

## When will I receive my security deposit after requesting a refund?

After the original membership period has ended and all books and outstanding charges have been settled, you can request your deposit refund.

Sphotik will process and return the eligible security deposit within **3 to 7 working days** after the refund request.

## Can I pause my membership?

Sphotik's current V1 policy does not provide a standard membership pause option.

We may introduce a pause option in the future as the service develops.

## Can membership prices change?

Yes. Sphotik may revise membership prices for future memberships and renewals.

A price change will not change the price of an already active membership for its current paid period.

## Which areas do you deliver to?

Delivery availability depends on the areas currently served by Sphotik and our available courier partners.

We will clearly show whether your address is serviceable before you place an order.

We plan to expand service areas gradually as the library grows.

## How do I contact Sphotik?

For questions, complaints, book requests, delivery issues, or membership support, use the official contact information provided on the Sphotik website.
