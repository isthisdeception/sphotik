# Sphotik Membership Rules

**Version:** V1  
**Last updated:** August 2026

These rules explain how Sphotik membership, borrowing, security deposits, delivery, returns, and book responsibility work.

Sphotik may update these rules from time to time as the service develops. Changes will apply to future memberships, renewals, and transactions unless otherwise stated.

---

## 1. Membership

A Sphotik membership gives a member access to the Sphotik library for the selected membership period.

### Membership options

| Membership | Regular Price | Founding Price |
|---|---:|---:|
| **6 Months** | ৳1,200 | **৳900** |
| **12 Months** | ৳2,000 | **৳1,600** |

The founding-member price is a launch offer and may not remain available permanently.

The membership fee is for **library access**. It does not include the security deposit or delivery charges.

Once a membership has been activated, the membership fee is non-refundable except where Sphotik explicitly agrees otherwise.

---

## 2. Security Deposit

A security deposit is required before a member can borrow books.

The security deposit is **separate from the membership fee** and is intended to protect the library against loss, serious damage, or other unresolved charges.

### Borrowing levels

| Security Deposit | Books at a Time | Maximum Total Book Value | Borrowing Period |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| **৳500** | Up to 2 books | Up to ৳700 | 10 days |
| **৳1,000** | Up to 4 books | Up to ৳1,400 | 14 days |
| **৳1,500** | Up to 6 books | Up to ৳2,000 | 21 days |

A member must remain within **both** their book-count limit and maximum total book-value limit.

The security deposit remains the member's money and is refundable subject to these rules.

---

## 3. Changing Borrowing Capacity

A member may increase their borrowing capacity by adding to their security deposit, subject to the available borrowing levels.

For example:

- ৳500 deposit → up to 2 books
- Increase to ৳1,000 → up to 4 books
- Increase to ৳1,500 → up to 6 books

To reduce a borrowing level and receive the additional deposit back, the member must first return enough books to meet the lower borrowing limit.

---

## 4. Borrowing Books

A member may borrow an available book when:

1. the book is currently available;
2. the member has remaining borrowing capacity; and
3. adding the book will not exceed the member's maximum total book-value limit.

Books that are currently borrowed by other members may be unavailable for immediate borrowing. Where applicable, Sphotik may provide a **waitlist** for unavailable books.

A member may not borrow additional books while outstanding books prevent them from meeting the applicable borrowing limits.

---

## 5. Borrowing Period

The borrowing period depends on the member's security-deposit level:

- **৳500 deposit:** 10 days
- **৳1,000 deposit:** 14 days
- **৳1,500 deposit:** 21 days

The borrowing period begins according to the date recorded by Sphotik when the book is issued to the member.

Sphotik may allow a renewal when there is no waiting member for the book.

Renewals are not guaranteed and may be restricted for popular or reserved books.

---

## 6. Delivery Charges

Delivery is **not included** in the membership fee.

The member is responsible for delivery charges for each physical trip.

### Outbound delivery

**Sphotik → Member**

The member pays the delivery charge when the books are sent to them.

### Return delivery

**Member → Sphotik**

The member pays the return delivery charge when books are sent back to Sphotik.

When multiple books are sent together in one shipment, they count as **one delivery trip**.

A member may use a Sphotik-arranged courier service or another return method approved by Sphotik.

---

## 7. Returning Books

Members must return borrowed books within their applicable borrowing period.

A member must return all borrowed books before borrowing additional books when their current borrowing capacity has been reached.

If a member expects to be late, they should contact Sphotik as soon as possible.

Sphotik may provide a grace period or extension at its discretion.

---

## 8. Late Returns

Late returns can prevent other members from borrowing books, particularly when a book is in demand.

Sphotik may temporarily suspend borrowing privileges when books are overdue.

Repeated or extended late returns may result in additional restrictions, charges, or suspension of membership.

Any late-return policy may be updated as Sphotik gains operational experience.

---

## 9. Book Condition and Normal Wear

Books are intended to be read and reasonable signs of normal use are acceptable.

Normal reading wear may include:

- slight cover wear;
- minor corner bending;
- ordinary page wear; and
- other reasonable signs of use.

Members are responsible for taking reasonable care of borrowed books.

Writing, highlighting, tearing pages, intentionally damaging covers, exposing books to water, or otherwise damaging a book beyond normal use is not permitted.

---

## 10. Damaged Books

If a book is returned with damage beyond normal wear, Sphotik may assess the damage and determine a reasonable repair or replacement charge.

Where applicable, the charge may be deducted from the member's security deposit.

Borrowing privileges may be suspended until the relevant amount has been settled.

---

## 11. Lost or Irreparably Damaged Books

If a member loses a book, or a book is damaged beyond reasonable repair, Sphotik may charge an appropriate replacement amount.

The amount may consider the book's current replacement cost, availability, condition, and other reasonable factors.

The applicable amount may be deducted from the member's security deposit.

If the amount due exceeds the available security deposit, the member remains responsible for paying the remaining amount.

---

## 12. Outstanding Books and Charges

A member may not borrow additional books while they have:

- overdue books;
- lost or seriously damaged books that have not been resolved;
- unpaid charges; or
- any other unresolved issue that prevents normal borrowing.

Sphotik may temporarily suspend borrowing privileges until the issue is resolved.

---

## 13. Security Deposit Refund

The security deposit is refundable when a member leaves Sphotik, provided that:

1. all borrowed books have been returned;
2. any applicable damage, loss, or other charges have been settled; and
3. there are no other outstanding obligations.

After the account is cleared, Sphotik will process the remaining refundable deposit according to its current refund procedure.

The refund may take a reasonable processing period depending on the payment method and internal verification process.

---

## 14. Membership Suspension or Cancellation

Sphotik may suspend or cancel a membership in cases including, but not limited to:

- repeated late returns;
- failure to return books;
- loss or serious damage to books without resolution;
- misuse of the service;
- fraudulent activity;
- providing false information; or
- serious or repeated violations of these rules.

In such cases, any refundable deposit will be handled according to the outstanding balance and applicable rules.

---

## 15. Member Information

Members are responsible for keeping their contact information accurate and up to date.

This includes, where applicable:

- name;
- phone number;
- delivery address; and
- email address.

Members should notify Sphotik when their information changes.

---

## 16. Account and Membership Use

A membership is intended for the registered member.

A member should not lend their account, borrowing privileges, or books to another person unless Sphotik has explicitly allowed it.

The member remains responsible for books borrowed through their account.

---

## 17. Early Cancellation and Security Deposit

A member may choose to stop using Sphotik before their membership period ends.

If a member cancels their membership early:

- The membership will no longer be available for use after cancellation.
- The membership fee will **not be refunded** for the unused period.
- The security deposit will **remain with Sphotik until the original membership period ends**.
- After the original membership period has ended, the member may request the return of their eligible security deposit.
- After the refund request is submitted, Sphotik will process and return the eligible security deposit within **3 to 7 working days**, provided that all borrowed books have been returned and all outstanding charges have been settled.

### Example

A member purchases a 6-month membership with a ৳1,000 security deposit.

After 3 months, the member decides to cancel.

The member's membership access will end, but the ৳1,000 security deposit will remain with Sphotik until the original 6-month membership period is completed.

After the 6-month period ends, the member can request the deposit refund. Sphotik will return the eligible amount within **3 to 7 working days**, provided there are no outstanding books or charges.


## 18. Changes to These Rules

Sphotik may revise its membership, borrowing, deposit, delivery, and operational rules as the service develops.

Any significant changes will be communicated through appropriate channels.

The rules applicable to a member may depend on when the membership or transaction was made.

---

## 19. Contact

For questions about membership, borrowing, deposits, returns, or other policies, please contact Sphotik through the official contact channels provided on the website.
