# How It Works

**The Process**

**Join once, borrow, read, return, and repeat.**

## 01 - Join Sphotik

Choose a 6-month or 12-month membership and place your refundable security deposit.

## 02 - Choose a book

Browse the library and choose a book that is currently available.

Can't find what you're looking for? Request it from us.

## 03 - Receive

We pack your books and send them to you.

You pay the delivery fee for the shipment.

## 04 - Read & return

Keep the books within your borrowing period, then send them back to us.

You pay the return delivery fee.

## 05 - Borrow again

Once your books are returned, your borrowing capacity becomes available again.

Choose your next book and repeat.
